DFLAB / Technical Project

Digital Forensics Laboratory

DFLAB is a technical project for documenting the design and development of a Digital Forensics Laboratory. Its scope includes infrastructure, hardware, evidence storage and management, tools, security, deployment, and operating procedures. Content and implementation status will be updated as project decisions and evidence are recorded.

Project Overview

A single place to plan and document a controlled forensic laboratory environment, its evidence-handling approach, and its technical design.

Objectives & Scope

Define laboratory requirements, document architecture and procurement decisions, establish evidence-management practices, and plan secure deployment. Specific capabilities and scope remain subject to documented project decisions.
